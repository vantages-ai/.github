```text
  1. PROVE (Free Endpoint Scan)
     Run a diagnostic at https://vantages.app/scan on your AI endpoint or manifest.
     Missing controls (e.g., Art. 14 oversight, /.well-known/poppy.json, Annex IV)?
                                      │
                                      ▼
  2. REMEDIATE (Cookbook Remix & Deterministic checks 7/7)
     Clone vantages-ai/governance-evidence ("Use this template") and route
     identified gaps through vantages-ai/acta-remix -> receives tested Remix PRs
     with RFC 8785 canonical digests and runs local/CI `checks 7/7`.
                                      │
                                      ▼
  3. ENFORCE & KEEP PROVING (Chamber Review -> Dynamic Living Passport)
     Submit your dossier for 48h Evidence Review (https://vantages.app/evidence-review).
     Accredited Chamber review (Article 14, Role >= 40) issues a PQC-countersigned
     Living Passport & dynamic Sigil badge. Runtime drift switches fail-closed to
     VOIDED_ON_DRIFT until human re-review restores green status.
```
